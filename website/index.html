Step inside your 3D videos.

Immivio is a free VR video player built for 3D and immersive video. Play side-by-side, top-bottom, VR180 and 360° files from your headset, NAS or media server — on a giant virtual screen or all around you.

Features

Built around the way you actually watch.

Your library

Bring the library you already have.

Open videos stored on the headset or a USB drive, or connect the servers already running at home. Every network source lives under one Network media entry.

  • Local files, USB drives and STRM shortcuts
  • SMB shared folders and DLNA media libraries
  • Plex, Emby, Jellyfin, Stash and XBVR servers
  • Only videos and folders, in list or icon view
Comfort

A screen that fits how you sit — or lie back.

Put the picture exactly where you want it, at the size you want it. Immivio remembers your layout for next time.

  • Size 5–3000%, distance 0.1–50 m, adjustable curvature
  • TV and cinema presets, reclining mode and head-follow
  • Window height, sharpness and display quality
  • Prefers 72 Hz where the headset supports it
Subtitles

Subtitles that work in 3D.

Text subtitles render in their own layer, so they stay sharp and readable on flat screens and immersive video alike.

  • SRT, ASS, SSA and VTT with automatic encoding detection
  • Two languages at once, with one shared delay
  • Distance, direction and position for immersive video
  • Captions below the screen for flat 3D video
And the details

Small things that add up.

One compact control bar

Speed, subtitles, color, timing, stereo alignment and the queue — each one popup away.

Playback queues

Sequential or shuffled, returning to the folder after the last video.

Remembers every video

Progress, tracks, stereo alignment and screen layout are kept per file.

Comfortable stereo

Swap eyes and fine-tune horizontal alignment until the 3D looks right.

Color presets

Original, Warm, Cool or Custom, with a reset beside every slider.

Four languages

English, Simplified Chinese, Traditional Chinese and Japanese.

No account. No ads. No tracking.

Your media, history and sign-ins stay on your headset or go only to the servers you connect. The source code is open for anyone to check.

Install in three steps

  1. Enable developer mode

    Turn on developer mode on the headset, connect it to your computer over USB and allow debugging.

  2. Install the APK

    Use any sideloading tool. Source ZIP/TAR archives on the releases page are not apps.

  3. Open Immivio

    Find it among unknown-source apps in the headset library. On PICO: Library → Unknown sources.

Performance depends on the headset, codec, bitrate and resolution. The OpenXR build needs a compatible Android runtime. Full installation guide →

FAQ

Before you press play.

Is Immivio really free?

Yes. Immivio is free to download, use and build, with no purchase or subscription. The source is on GitHub under GPL-3.0. Third-party media services follow their own terms.

Which headsets are supported?

PICO (Neo3 and later) and Meta Quest (Quest 2 and later) each have their own build. An experimental OpenXR build targets other Android headsets with a compatible OpenXR runtime.

My 3D video looks split, doubled or distorted.

Check the projection first (Flat, 180°, Fisheye or 360°), then the stereo layout (mono, SBS or TB), then try swapping eyes. For VR180 SBS fisheye files, choose Fisheye + SBS. See the format table →

Which formats and subtitles work?

Flat, 180°, 360° and fisheye video in mono, side-by-side or top-bottom stereo, including half-width and half-height packing. Subtitles must be text (SRT, ASS, SSA or VTT); bitmap subtitles are not offered. MV-HEVC and Dolby Vision colour accuracy have not been validated yet.

Will 8K video play smoothly?

That depends on the codec, bitrate, projection and headset. If playback stutters, try a lower-bitrate version of the file first.

Will a new version update my installed app?

Only when the application ID and signing key match. Builds from other distributors, and some CI builds, use different keys and cannot update each other.

How is Immivio related to Thru3D Media Player?

Immivio continues from version 0.6.0 of zerochocobo/Thru3D-Media-Player and has been developed independently by NorthBoundWisdom since then. Report issues for Immivio in this project's repository.
