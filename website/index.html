Learn the everyday controls →
  1. Put it in place

    Hold the controller grip to move a picture or window; use the same hand’s thumbstick to adjust distance where supported.

  2. Settle in

    Watch seated, or enable reclining mode. Follow is a separate setting, so you can choose how the view behaves.

    Per-file viewing preferences, timestamp bookmarks and saved playback queues make it easier to return to your media.

Good to know

A few things before you press play.

AI or No AI — which should I choose?

No AI is a smaller install for ordinary videos, photos and existing stereo media. AI adds depth and person matting for supported content; results depend on models and GPU support. Playback backends also differ, so check the specific build for PGS, spatial and Dolby capabilities.

How do I install it on my headset?

Enable developer mode, connect to your computer and approve USB debugging, then install the APK with your preferred sideloading tool. Open Thru3D from the headset’s sideloaded-app library. Follow the installation guide →

Why does my video look split or distorted?

Match the projection and stereo layout to the source. Use Flat for ordinary video; use Fisheye and SBS for VR180 SBS fisheye media. Projection, side-by-side/top-bottom packing, and eye order are separate choices.

How is this related to the original project?

This edition continues from the original project’s 0.6.0 baseline and follows its own development and release path. NorthBoundWisdom maintains it independently. Report issues for this edition in our repository.
